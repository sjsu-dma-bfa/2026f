JUPITER KASLER

Artist Statement / Bio

Jupiter is a digital media artist with a focus on net art, tech art, and digital illustration. Most of their works and upcoming projects focus on LGBTQ identity, relationships, and dealing with regular every day problems. Their hope is that you will enjoy their works and make whatever interpretation of it that you will.

Artwork Statement

This work is about long distance relationships. I've been in one for a long time, and never before had I considered visa costs, or plane tickets, or how much staying up late affects function the next day just to spite the time zone I was in. It was difficult, but I did it. This work was made to represent that struggle. Waiting just for those few minutes of interaction. Some days we don't talk, and some we barely do. I wait for the days where we talk the most.
