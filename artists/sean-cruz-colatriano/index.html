I'm a digital media artist that's created work in lots of different mediums. Recently I've developed a practice in digital aesthetics utilizing Touch Designer and After Effects. Project

The Human After

25 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork statement

The Human After is a look into the long-term consequences of humanity’s devotion to push technology further. It imagines a future where our society’s consumption of digital systems and hardware creates an indefinite dependency on technology. The video piece breaks the human form into unrecognizable shapes in a chaotic digital landscape- presenting the idea of losing oneself in the pursuit of advancement. Society’s addiction to progressing technology is indistinguishable from erosion; neural decay. The sculpture embodies an exaggerative aftermath of integration. The body becomes a host to hardware and its organic systems replaced by circuitry- a grotesque fusion that blurs the definition of what it means to be human. Our reality becomes a dream, swapping it with virtual experiences that provide comfort to the horrors of what was real; eternal escape. These tools and technologies meant to assist, morph into dependency; twisting further into necessity. It silently reshapes us until it’s too late.
