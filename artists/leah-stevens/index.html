LEAH STEVENS

Fragments

Artist statement / bio

Leah Stevens’s work explores personal memory, identity, and the connections people build through art. Working primarily with digital illustration, animation, and graphic design, she draws inspiration from everyday experiences and nostalgic memories that have shaped how she sees herself and relates to others. Growing up, creating became a way for Stevens to express herself and share something personal with the people around her. As her interests and experiences have changed, her art has grown alongside them. She sees her art practice as a continuous process of discovering who she is and how her identity may evolve in the future. Using tools such as Procreate and After Effects, Stevens combines hand-drawn imagery with structured compositions. Her work brings digital art into physical spaces, creating opportunities for viewers to connect with her memories and reflect on the experiences that have shaped their own identities.

Project

Fragments

17 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork statement

"Fragments" is a short, looping animation that pieces together significant memories of the artist growing up with art. Hand-drawn in Procreate and animated in After Effects, these scenes trace how creativity has shaped her identity and current work. Projected into a physical picture frame, the animation brings preserved moments into motion, reflecting how quickly time passes while memories continue to influence the present. Through this collection of personal experiences, "Fragments" invites viewers to reflect on their own formative memories and the role art or creativity has played in their lives.
