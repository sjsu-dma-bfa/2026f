EMILY CHAING

CTRL/ALT+SELF*

Artist Statement / Bio

My art practice explores how we discover ourselves through the emotions, identities, and stories embedded in the smallest things around us. Through observation, storytelling, and imagination, I create animal-like characters, each with its own personality and connection to social issues. I draw inspiration from my surroundings, particularly nature, exploring the contrasts and connections between the physical world and the digital realm. My work invites viewers into a reflective and imaginative space where they can form emotional connections, interpret the narratives in their own ways, and bring their personal experiences into the work. I am interested in how art can communicate different perspectives and create meaningful connections between individuals, society, and nature. Working across digital drawing, sculpture, and video, I experiment with the relationships between digital and physical media, exploring how each influences and transforms the other. Through these processes, I use artistic expression to communicate ideas, question perspectives, and explore how different forms of media shape the ways we experience and understand the world.

Project

CTRL/ALT+SELF*

08 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

CTRL/ALT+SELF* is a short story about the two characters. One focusing on the physical and mental state, while the second character focuses on the digital disembodiment. I chose the topic of focusing on the idea of digital disembodiment because as technology continues to advances we tend to lose ourselves without realizing it. In the topic of focusing on digital disembodiment I also wanted to focus on our physical and mental state. Growing up can be hard, especially as time keeps going by and it feels like it is going too fast. Sometimes wishing time would stop and take in the moment to just enjoy it. Also learning about responsibility while still being able to have fun and being yourself.
