ROSALVA JIMÉNEZ MARTÍNEZ

Mural Vivo (Living Mural)

Artist Statement / Bio

Rosalva Jimenez Martinez is a San Jose–based digital artist who loves exploring identity, culture, and mental health through her art. Drawn to the space where her Mexican-American roots meet technology, she brings her ideas to life using digital drawing, 3D modeling, and creative coding to express feelings and stories that are often hard to put into plain words. Inspired by traditional Mexican storytelling, Rosalva brings heritage into the modern digital world. In her free time, she finds everyday inspiration listening to music, sketching on her iPad, and hanging out with her family and friends; keeping her creative process grounded in personal connection. Mural Vivo (Living Mural) Mural Vivo (Spanish for Living Mural) is a digital animation loop that brings traditional Mexican storytelling into the modern digital world. At the center of the piece is Rosalva herself, surrounded by colorful papel picado, bright cempasúchil (Marigold) flowers, and monarch butterflies fluttering along a glowing animated path. The piece holds a deeply personal tribute to her late cat, Michi, reimagined as a vibrant alebrije—a fantastical spirit guide watching over her and serving as a constant source of creative inspiration. Through its continuous loop, the mural comes alive as a space where memory, love, and culture flow together without end.

Artwork Statement

Mural Vivo (Spanish for Living Mural) is a digital animation loop that brings traditional Mexican storytelling into the modern digital world. At the center of the piece is Rosalva herself, surrounded by colorful papel picado, bright cempasúchil (Marigold) flowers, and monarch butterflies fluttering along a glowing animated path. The piece holds a deeply personal tribute to her late cat, Michi, reimagined as a vibrant alebrije—a fantastical spirit guide watching over her and serving as a constant source of creative inspiration. Through its continuous loop, the mural comes alive as a space where memory, love, and culture flow together without end.
