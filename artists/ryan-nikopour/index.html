RYAN NIKOPOUR

InterOdyssey

Artist Statement / Bio

Ryan Nikopour is a multidisciplinary video game designer and software engineer studying Digital Media Art and Computer Science at San Jose State University, working at the intersection of design, code, and digital media. His practice spans UI/UX design, software engineering, game development, and 3D/graphic design, striving to always push the boundaries between technical precision and artistic elegance. With a deep passion for abstract aesthetics and digital experimentation, he creates experiences that challenge prexisting conventional design paradigms. Whether building interactive web applications, designing game environments, or crafting visual narratives, his approach to each project is perceived as an opportunity to merge form with function. His work draws inspiration from brutalist architecture, NPR, glitch art, cyberpunk culture, and the raw beauty of code itself. He believes the most compelling digital experiences emerge when we embrace the tension between order and chaos.

Artwork Statement

InterOdyssey is an action-rougelike taking place in a distant alternate future that spreads across the vast bounds of the universe. Take control of a forsaken pilot and restore the fabric of reality while unraveling the secrets of a forgotten prolonged war.
