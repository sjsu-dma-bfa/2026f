KYRA JONES

REV:\2199

Artist Statement / Bio

Kyra Jones is a digital artist who specializes in illustration and other forms of creative media including photography, animation, and digital video. Much of her work is inspired by internalized feelings of melancholy, angst, determination and reserve. She uses her practice as a way to envision and navigate her lived experiences, personal interests, and cognitive rationale.

Project

REV:\2199

15 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

Inspired by the prophetic messaging in the book of Revelations, REV:\\2199 is an animated digital video sequence that delivers cinematic immersion and explores creative theory. Instead of being set in an era long past, the viewer is transported to an ultramodern setting with a futuristic lens and baroquian themes. The work blends historical, religious, and technological interpretations. REV:\\2199 is the finished product of a variety of digital mediums, meticulously designed using software such as Blender, Adobe After Effects, TouchDesigner, and original 3D and 2D artwork and assets.
