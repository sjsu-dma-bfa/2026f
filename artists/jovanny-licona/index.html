JOVANNY LICONA

fly on the wall

Artist statement / bio

I am a fly on the wall. My illustrations are a mix of abstract and realism. Ever since I was little, my art has worked as a projection of what I interpreted and the many influences around me. Using the two video formats displayed in front of you, I wish to demonstrate not only an intangible image correlating with the screen but also connections between the conscious and unconscious. One screen shows what is seen at the moment; the other holds what the whole moment is, and the two are linked, so every selection on one echoes on the other. While ur fouse might be drawn to the screen in front of our brain are on the move. Our minds wander through memory, instinct, and interpretation, making connections we never knowingly choose. To create, regardless of whether anybody sees my work; I just want to create. No audience is required and no approval needed. The act of making is the purpose, and the work is a quiet record of how I see.
