BRANDON VUONG

“Where’s My Face?”

Artist Statement / Bio

Brandon Vuong is a digital media artist based in San Jose that combines 3-D modeling with traditional sculpture-making techniques that highlight the relationship between the immaterial sculpting methods within the physical space. He experiments with concepts that are derived from universal cultural experiences and personal displacement in his practice.

Artwork Statement

“Where’s My Face?” is a 3D printed sculptural piece that opens up the conversation of the cultural disconnect between the first American-born children and their immigrant parents. Being in a position where his parents were required to assimilate in order to survive in unfamiliar lands, the piece represents Brandon and the confusion he faces in his pursuit of cultural identity where he is taught traditional values within the home but taught American culture in public spaces. “His lips speak the broken language his face phyically represents.”
