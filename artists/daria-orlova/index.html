DARIA ORLOVA

Rift

Artist Statement / Bio

Daria Orlova is a Digital Media Artist that is driven by process and technical curiosity, exploring how digital media can recontextualize the organic world and uncover its underlying systems. With a better understanding of nature's complexity, she believes that new digital realities can be created. In collaborative projects, much of her work lies in solving practical problems through creative approaches of engineering. She is passionate about working with other artists to plan group installations and bring engaging artworks to life. Daria hopes her work inspires others to try something new and spark their own creative curiosities.

Project

Rift

05 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

What lies on the edges of this reality? Through "Rift", viewers make contact with a new unknown. As visitors peer into the broken remains of a TV, they may find that the observer becomes the observed. Created using TouchDesigner and Blender, the world in the "Rift" calls on human reactions of murky waters and looming creatures, as well as, tense curiosity. The real-time video and 3D modeled environment are controlled through depth-based computer vision that reacts to the movement of the viewer.
