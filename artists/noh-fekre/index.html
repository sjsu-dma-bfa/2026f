NOH FEKRE

Long Week

Artist statement / bio

Noh is a digital media artist who has a focus on video editing. While he mainly focuses on video editing, he has some level of experience with most adobe products including photoshop and 3D programs such as blender and many other mediums as he tries to find one that really speaks to him. His work often involves remixing different pre-existing things like music, audio cues, animation, pre-recorded visuals and ideas in an attempt to make something new out of it or to provide a different angle on it. His art work often varies widely from piece to piece but he sometimes tries to incorporate or reference media he is interested in as they often serve as some kind of inspiration for the art pieces he wants to make. In remixing things, his work is often somewhat referential and hopefully that elicits a feeling of familiarity within the audience while also providing something new or a new view point on that feeling. Project

Long Week

21 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork statement

This piece is meant to offer an exaggerated version of "nostalgia” and to test what evokes that feeling within people. Is it enough for the subject to be something you're vaguely familiar with or does the video/picture/event have to be something you're intimately familiar with to evoke that feeling? Do filters, even ones that aren’t really accurate to the time period like applying black and white and grain to a digital video, assist in making that feeling stronger? Does it make it feel older than it is? [6]
