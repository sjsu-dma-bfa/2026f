ZUKA ISTANBOOY

The Architecture of Being

Artist statement / bio

Zuka Istanbooly is a multimedia artist, whose work explores the relationship between human experience, nature, identity, and technology. Drawing inspiration from the natural world and the often overlooked details of everyday life, she creates work that invites curiosity, reflection, and a deeper appreciation of the world around us. Her practice spans traditional and digital mediums, including painting, photography, set design, 3D modeling, and interactive technologies. Through color, light, texture, symbolism, and visual distortion, she translates intangible emotions and experiences into visual forms that bridge the physical and digital worlds. With her art, she hopes to invite viewers to pause, look closer, and rediscover wonder in the familiar. The Architecture of Being

Project

The Architecture of Being

31 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork statement

The Architecture of Being is an archival portrait of a person’s identity captured through 3D Gaussian splatting technology. It explores the idea that under the pressure to be legible to others, one condenses themselves into a box with only a few things that they use to represent themselves. Yet, identity is layered, shifting, and impossible to fully represent or contain. We are shaped by so many things that whatever you put into that box is only a fragment of a much larger story. This project acknowledges that sentiment, and plays on the juxtaposition of making that box, filling it with all we can, and recognizing that this is merely a portrait frozen in time of a person’s identity. As the timeline goes backwards or forward and from one person to another, the contents of that box may change reflecting values, interests, cultural heritage, struggles, memories, and dreams.
