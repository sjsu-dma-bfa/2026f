EVELYN (EVEE) PALMENO

Passage of Time

Artist Statement / Bio

Passage of time is an art piece dedicated to my sisters, my parents and my life as the youngest sibling growing up as well as having a large age gap of close to 20 years between myself and my oldest sister. Growing up in a family of seven which is made up of my parents, my four older sisters (no brothers) and myself the large age gap was interesting to say the least as it felt at some moments like I was an only and sometimes lonely child at times due to my sisters being more grown and independent. Growing up, some things have felt inconsistent, whereas others felt constant. Strangely enough, a constant feeling and motivation, was seeing my sister Cynthia’s 1965 Ford Mustang and knowing my sister’s dedication and work to restore her car. The characters in this short digital 2D animation about the passage of time is a very loose interpretation of myself and my family. Where the passage of time is what the title entails, time moves regardless of what happens and the only way is forward and sometimes feels like it repeats but you can never go back to the exact moment you has once before.

Artwork Statement

Brief artist statement: The passage of time affects all of us as we begin so young and some of us age so old. Everything in life changes but there are some things that stay same. This passage of time is expressed in this digital artwork, as every passing second creates a beautiful moment, memory and only moving forward. Instagram: eveeningart
