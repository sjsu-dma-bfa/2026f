YANHENG MA

Still Here

Artist Statement / Bio

This piece is an animated short reel that dives into my relationship with memories, social media, and the broader virtual world. "Still Here" is a personal piece that is created solely for my own personal cherishing, but I now want to share it with the world. I was originally inspired by reels of motion graphic edits of real recorded life scenes that portrayed people lives. From people exploring nature to showing off their fashion to awesome edits of great people that inspired them. As I push towards graduation I realized all the amazing memories that were like recorded life scenes that were ingrained into my memory and my heart. That was when I decided to pursue "Still Here" which is a mix-media piece involving live action motion graphics. Starting out I had many inspirations, in fact way too many. I scouted the internet from Instagram reels to reddit posts and all over Pinterest too figure out a "style" that fitted what I wanted to do. Initially I wanted to create a piece that was heavy with glitch art to represent the digital aspects that I wanted to really make stand out. However I felt that it would be underrepresenting all the good and the "memory part" of the piece that I really wanted to swing a home run on. So I focused more on a comic style approach with big bold letters and tons of representations of superheroes in comics. I really explored this idea further and see my piece as an exploration of my past through the lens of reading a comic book.
