NGAN VU

Fragments of Home

Artist Statement / Bio

My art explores the relationship between memory, identity, culture, and the idea of home. Growing up in Vietnam and later moving to the United States has shaped the way I see the world and how I express myself through art. I am especially interested in how personal experiences and childhood memories can influence our sense of identity and belonging. As a Digital Media Art student, I enjoy experimenting with different materials and techniques to bring my ideas to life. My creative process often involves combining digital and traditional art forms, allowing me to explore storytelling in different ways. I am drawn to everyday places and small details that may seem ordinary but hold personal meaning. Through my work, I want to capture feelings of nostalgia and the connection between the past and present. I believe that even as we grow older and experience new environments, our memories remain an important part of who we are. My goal as an artist is to create work that feels personal while encouraging others to reflect on their own experiences, cultural backgrounds, and the places they consider home. Fragments of homes

Project

Fragments of Home

20 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

Fragments of Home is a layered shadow box inspired by the alley in District 10, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, where I spent my childhood. Using hand-drawn illustrations, 3D-printed objects, and lighting, I recreate the familiar streets, houses, motorbikes, and small details that remind me of home. Each layer represents a different part of my memory, creating depth and a sense of nostalgia. This piece reflects my experience of leaving Vietnam and growing up in another country while still holding onto my roots. Through this work, I hope to preserve a place that remains meaningful to me.
