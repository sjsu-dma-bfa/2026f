TRISTAN HUYNH

Devil in the Details

Artist Statement / Bio

As someone who has always thought themselves an entertainer, I always want to strive to create an interesting experience for the viewer that they will always remember. In this I want to combine both my love for gaming, with my love of creating immersive experiences for other people. This game is something of the cumulative abilities I have gained since I joined SJSU. Long have I attempted to figure out what it is that makes me an artist, What is my expression? With this question in mind, I want to really add another layer to the usual digital experience of a DMA- like show by adding a more “real” element to the mix. W This exhibition represents my long desires and dreams as both artist and entertainer. That I was able to create a real video game and a real adventure for someone to have that creates a truly immersive time.

Artwork Statement

Devil in the Details is a pixel-art game about working in a supernatural insurance office. The player reads emails, investigates claims, checks company policies, and decides whether someone deserves help. I wanted the setting to feel ordinary at first, but increasingly wrong the longer you look at it. The limited colors, sparse details, and strange evidence are inspired by unsettling low-resolution horror games. There is also some humor, because dealing with monsters probably still involves paperwork, bad emails, and coworkers who complain too much.
