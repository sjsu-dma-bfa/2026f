LAUREN ANDERSON

Behind the Mask

Artist Statement / Bio

Lauren is a Digital Media Art student pursuing her Bachelor's degree with a concentration in 3D modeling and illustration. Her creative practice draws from her lived experiences, using visual storytelling to explore personal themes, perspectives, and reflections on life.

Project

Behind the Mask

16 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

This piece centers on the concept of masking, or presenting a version of oneself that aligns with societal expectations while concealing genuine emotions. Drawing from her own experiences, Lauren uses this artwork to express the challenges of maintaining an outward appearance that does not always reflect her internal reality.
