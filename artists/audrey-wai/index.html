AUDREY WAI

Missed Connections

Artist Statement / Bio

Audrey Wai is a digital artist who dabbles in a little bit of everything. From Blender and animation to ceramics and stop motion. She is an experimenter above all and likes to find different mediums to create and play with. Before her academic journey as an artist, Audrey was a scientist. As a child, Audrey always participated in arts and crafts, such as taking art classes or making gifts for others. She was artistically inclined and always leaned towards creative endeavors. However, Audrey wanted to follow in her father's footsteps academically and pursue Biology, even going so far as to attend his alma mater, the University of California, Berkeley. However, before attending UC Berkeley, Audrey decided that Biology was no longer her path and that she wanted to pursue something else. And that something else was art. She studied art for a year at UC Berkeley before deciding the school wasn't for her. From there, she decided she wanted to pursue digital art at San Jose State. Now she has fun exploring different mediums and figuring out what she wants to tackle next.

Artwork Statement

Missed Connections is a Blender animation about the fragility of human connections. Two blobs-one organic and one angular-represent people whose differences make connection difficult. As they reach across distance, time zones, and everyday life, they send fragments of themselves to the other. With every piece sent, they grow smaller and dimmer, revealing vulnerability and exhaustion. Despite their missed connections, they eventually connect. They glow brighter and larger than before-not because they've become the same, but because they've learned how to meet each other. Through light, form, and movement, Missed Connections explores the growing pains, sacrifices, and joy of human connection.
