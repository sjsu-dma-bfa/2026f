KATY YURASH

Toon to Life

Artist Statement / Bio

Katy Yurash is an illustrator, stop motion, and digital media artist from Mountain View, CA. Katy's artwork is inspired by 90s cartoon aesthetic and 2000s nostalgia. She combines a bold & vibrant color palette with original characters in her creative art practice.

Project

Toon to Life

14 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

Toon to Life is a multi-media short film that combines Claymation, 2D animation, and live action video. Animation is this artist's live long passion. It is why she became an artist in the first place. So for her final project, she wanted to bring her original cartoon character to life!
