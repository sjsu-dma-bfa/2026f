ANDREA ARELLANO GARCIA

Impermanence

Artist Statement / Bio

My work is heavily based on creating dream-like, surreal settings, no matter the medium. Through character design and illustration, I can create stories with minimal words. A setting and story can sometimes be understood with just images, and that's a beautiful thing. Even in my more personal artworks that touch on more serious topics, I prefer to have some things stay surreal and somewhat vague to allow room for personal interpretation from viewers. All my work experiments with color to aid in setting a mood for the piece; it is intentional and implies emotion without having to state it directly through words or cliché depictions

Project

Impermanence

02 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

Impermanence is a short animated film, focusing on life and mortality. It takes viewers through a visual journey of my fear of mortality and how I try to cope and come to terms with how temporary life is. It touches on different stages of life, religion, and incorporates parts of my culture that heavily revolve around the deceased. The film has a hand-drawn art style, resembling graphite on paper, to honor the medium that started my love for art.
