JOCELYN MONREAL-VELAZQUEZ

Into the Garden

Artist Statement / Bio

Jocelyn Monreal-Velazquez is a Mexican digital media artist based in San Jose, California, whose work is rooted in a passion for storytelling through design. Focusing on graphic design, her practice explores the relationship between visuals and interaction across immersive spaces and digital compositions. Guided by an imaginative approach, and inspired by technology and the communities around her, Jocelyn creates inclusive visual experiences that transform narratives into dynamic, emotionally engaging forms.

Project

Into the Garden

11 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

Into the Garden is an immersive projection installation inspired by the women who have shaped Jocelyn's life and the memories she carries of them. Her grandmother, mother, older sister, and younger sister are each represented by a specific flower connected to her memories and realtionship with them. Rather than representing herself through a specific flower, she exists through the greenery surrounding them, reflecting her preference for existing quietly. As a private person, she tends to keep many memories and emotions to herself. Some are difficult, while others are moments she values deeply and wants to protect. Instead of directly showing the women or explaining their individual stories, she uses flowers to express the emotions connected to these relationships, allowing her to share something personal while keeping parts of those memories private. Jocelyn, transfoms botanical footage through repetition, layering, fragmentation, distortion, and. pixel art to create surreal and abstract interpretations of the flowers. Growing up, she wathced her grandmother create and sell her needlework, while inspired by her own love for the craft. By using pixel art that resembles individual stiches, she brings a part of her grandmother's artistic influence to her own digital practice.
