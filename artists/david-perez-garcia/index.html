DAVID PEREZ GARCIA

Climate Destination

Artist Statement / Bio

David Perez Garcia has been interested/pursuing digital art, from a wide range such as 3D model, photoshop/illustrator, video and coding throughout his life. He grew up throughout his life in California where most of his family stay, not yet traveling outside of California but he hoped he would have a chance to. He graduated high school in DCP El Primero, which he is currently in San Jose State University majoring in Digital Media Art career path/major. Throughout childhood, David always had a spark of passion in the world of art/digital art of seeking what is the purpose of everything in art but also life. Now, he has learned a wide range of digital art from video edit, glitch art, coding, photoshop/illustrator and most importantly for 3D modeling. 3D modeling has been his biggest passion from not only making creative models but also giving purpose and meaning to each and every model. He is driven to improve himself to not only get better at 3D modeling and share his own ideas/passion in the art community,

Artwork Statement

Climate Destination collects the issue and effect of climate change toward polar bears and fish within their environment. David uses this information to create his own unique way to express these issues to the word in ways others may have not seen. Creating bizarre and horror which may come in the future for these creatures. In hopes to not only bring attention but as well enlighten for those who become numb to climate change over the years through David passion.
