PHIN NGUYEN

The Fruits of My Labor

Artist Statement / Bio

Born in Hue, Vietnam, Phin Nguyen is a multidisciplinary artist, creative director, character designer, and projection mapper. Phin’s work is often recognized for its illustrative layers of graphics, signature posthumanist concepts, female figures, and metallic sigils, with visual references to Gen-Z media, fashion, and design, representing the abstract nuance of human emotions, identity, and experiences. Her practice is rooted in a Buddhist-spiritual mindset, a feminist lens, and a contemporary sense of Vietnamese girlhood. To Phin, she makes art for the feelings that are hard to explain, as she invites you to find vulnerability as you navigate through the welcoming layers and spaces of her artworks beyond the spectacle. Based in California, she is currently the creative director of Asterisk Magazine Issue 6, a graduating Digital Media Art BFA student at SJSU, and a solo live projection mapper across art institutions and spaces in Downtown San Jose and the Bay Area.

Project

The Fruits of My Labor

22 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

The Fruits of My Labor is a projection mapping installation that guides the audience through an immersive space and performance, consisting of a narrated audiovisual and a glowing starfruit tree – a direct reference to the one in the artist’s childhood home in Hue, Vietnam. The installation is a celebration of the end of a long journey, where she invites everyone to give space to all the lessons and those who have been a part of it. “Usually, I see artists make art about their struggles and trauma; I was no exception. However, it’s time I make art that celebrates.”
