DENIELLE SAMBILAY

Stay

Artist Statement / Bio

Denielle Sambilay is a Bay Area-based visual development and concept artist whose work is driven by a passion for storytelling and world-building. Her work explores character and environment design, as well as background painting. Influenced by animation, film, and games, Denielle incorporates elements of imagination, nostalgia, and personal connection into her projects. Through her art, she hopes to tell stories that resonate with audiences, drawing from her imagination, personal experiences, and the world around her. [1]

Artwork Statement

Stay is an animated installation that explores the life of a guard dog named Marty through a fixed, unmoving camera centered around his cage. The installation takes an observational approach, showing Marty’s everyday life within the limited space he is chained to through repetition.
