WENYU LIN

To See The Waves

Artist Statement / Bio

Wenyu Lin is a digital media artist based in the Bay Area, specializing in digital and video art. His work explores the dynamic interplay between abstract forms of expression and digital media, that transforms ideas into visuals. Through his artworks, Wenyu aims to capture and convey emotions, and personal experiences.

Project

To See The Waves

29 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork Statement

This project is all about using that sound data to transform a visual experience in video and bringing this invisible energy into view. Inspired by everyday experiences, To See the Waves treats the sounds we hear daily as a material. By converting audio signals into data signals, this work explores and experiments with the abstract visualization of sound and offers audiences their own relatable experience.
