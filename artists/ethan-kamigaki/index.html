ETHAN KAMIGAKI

The Mind Palace

Artist statement / bio

Ethan Kamigaki is a Digital Media Artist focused on 3D modeling, sculpting, and digital illustration. He designs unique characters and environments influenced by his own introspection, striving to tell stories that immerse viewers and connect with them emotionally through a single image. He hopes to evoke the same feelings he felt when first conceptualizing the piece. Furthermore, Ethan works to broaden his skill set by working in different mediums such as charcoal, video editing, and augmented reality. Across his works, Ethan hopes to connect with the viewer and ignite their curiosity, so that even after they leave, they ask themselves what else exists outside of the single frame, continuing the story in their own mind.

Project

The Mind Palace

09 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork statement

The Mind Palace is a virtual reality experience set in a surreal, dreamlike environment based on the artist’s inner thoughts. It’s a visualization of the artist’s mind and three prominent factors of stress in their life: procrastination, expectations, and anxiety. Split into three separate rooms, each factor is represented through a metaphorical experience that reflects how each one makes them feel. Combined, they create an introspective virtual reality experience that explores how stress can manifest differently from person to person.
