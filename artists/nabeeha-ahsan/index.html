NABEEHA AHSAN

It's Loud in Here

Artist statement / bio

Nabeeha is an interdisciplinary artist who enjoys portraying different types of visuals in conjunction with each other, whether by using opposing subject matter or media altogether. She uses this juxtaposition as a tool in efforts to reveal greater meaning than a single visual would have on its own, manifesting as a method of stimulating deeper thought about time, mental health, and global issues. For this reason, she gravitates towards collaging, not only in physical work, but also in her video and interactive artwork. Her merging of ideas in her work bleeds into her love of collaboration, as she constantly looks for ways in which different perspectives and possibilities can come together into a conceptual vision.

Project

It's Loud in Here

19 / Digital [afterimage]

Artwork statement

The project combines sculpture, digital visuals, sound, and interactive technology within a single installation. It is designed for one viewer at a time, creating a private encounter with the work. The installation asks the viewer to lean toward the door, look through a narrow opening, reach into an unseen space, and control a scene without seeing the controller directly. These actions make the viewer’s body part of the meaning. Behind focuses on the space between wanting to connect and wanting to withdraw. It presents anxiety and depression as conditions shaped through the body, where movement becomes difficult, control feels uncertain, and escape remains out of reach. Nabeeha uses projected mixed media analog animation in the installation ‘It’s Loud in Here’, in which she highlights the importance of speaking up even when it is easier to stay silent and turn the other way — especially when everyone else wants you to. She utilizes hand-drawn and physical components with news footage to highlight specific moments of tragedy, resilience, and joy in Palestine, serving as a spotlight on the lives being lived and lost during this time.
